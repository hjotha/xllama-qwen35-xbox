Date: 2026-09-28. Host: HPBEATS. Console: Xbox Series X at 192.168.1.26.
Target host: GOKAYA at 192.168.1.57. This records implementation and measured
results for the first GPU milestone of the [eight-stage plan](../dflash-gpu-implementation-plan-2026-09-28.md).

The production RPC protocol is still DFL2 v2 with fused SYNC_TRIM and TCP_NODELAY.
GPU opcodes 8–12 are isolated diagnostics; no result below is DFlash generation
tokens/second. The Xbox app keeps `n_gpu_layers=0` for SYNC and DRAFT.

that observation does not identify the crash cause or rule out GPU failure.
The older 1.0.0.13 DirectML Q4_K JSON claimed `passed` without a per-run
accuracy gate and is excluded from the comparison.

## Decision and remaining gates

HLSL over packed GGUF is the measured candidate for the tested Q3_K, Q4_K
and Q5_K matrices. The Q4_K DirectML variant is numerically valid but slower
with readback and uses 3.56 times the weight storage. These results justify
continuing to F3/F4; they do not show an end-to-end throughput gain.

The next implementation must keep weights and KV on GPU, port SYNC and DRAFT
including dynamic convolution and all F32/non-matmul operators, then validate
hidden states and rejection/rewind sequences. Only then can F5 fallback and
F6 interleaved Swift/CPU/GPU TPS comparisons answer whether the full RPC
beats the prior 12.81 tok/s CPU RPC and historical 34.99 tok/s Swift baseline.
